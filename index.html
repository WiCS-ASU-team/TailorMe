Getting Started

  1. Pick your kind of help. Mend & Hem, Fit & Tailor, or Made from Scratch.
  2. Find a maker. Browse tailor profiles, ratings, and sample work.
  3. Share your measurements. Fill out the guided form once and send it with a click.
  4. Say hello. Chat about fabric, fit, timing, and your budget.

Measurement Guide

Use a soft measuring tape, keep it snug without pulling tight, and measure over light clothing.

Pricing & Quotes

Every tailor lists a starting price. Your final quote depends on the garment, fabric, and amount of work, so message a tailor with your idea and measurements to get one.

This prototype doesn’t process payments or send real messages.

FAQs

How do I get a quote?

Open a tailor’s messages, describe what you need, and share your saved measurements. The tailor replies with a quote.

Do I need to know my measurements first?

No. The Measurement Guide walks you through each one, and you can save them to reuse with any tailor.

Can I choose more than one tailor?

Yes. Message as many tailors as you like before deciding who’s the best fit.

Is my information stored securely?

In this prototype, your measurements and chats are saved only in your own browser. There are no accounts or servers yet.

How can I become a tailor?

Visit our About Us page to learn how to join as a tailor.

ABOUT US

Made with care, for a wardrobe that feels like you.

Our Story

TailorMe began as a student team project at Arizona State University. We noticed that custom and well-fitted clothing often feels out of reach, while talented local tailors and seamstresses struggle to find new customers. We built TailorMe to bring the two together in one friendly place.

Our Mission

To make skilled tailoring affordable and accessible, so everyone can wear clothing that fits their measurements, style, and budget, while supporting local makers in the Tempe and Phoenix community.

Become a Tailor

Are you a tailor, seamstress, or student designer? Joining TailorMe is simple:

  1. Create a free profile with your name, location, and specialties.
  2. Share photos of your past work and set your starting prices.
  3. Receive messages and measurements from customers looking for your skills.
